title: Facilitating India's Largest Student Program - The 30 Days of Google Cloud

The blog entails the experience and learnings taken from facilitating the `The 30 Days of Google Cloud Program`. The post reflects on the experience of leading and facilitating the the program, which is a a community-driven initiative aimed at introducing students and professionals to cloud computing, machine learning, and it's infrastructure. 

The article serves as a roadmap for community leaders, detailing the balance between technical instruction and organizational management required to scale a campus-wide learning program.
